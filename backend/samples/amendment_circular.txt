SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA
CIRCULAR
SEBI/HO/MIRSD/DEMO/P/CIR/2026/117                                   November 6, 2026

To,
All Stock Brokers registered with SEBI

Subject: Amendment to Master Circular on Log Retention and Access Control for Stock Brokers

1. SEBI, vide circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/DEMO/P/CIR/2026/101 dated November 1, 2026, issued the Master Circular on Log Retention and Access Control for Stock Brokers. Based on feedback from market participants, the following amendments are made.

2. In clause 3.1 of the said Master Circular, the words "a minimum period of 2 years" shall be substituted with "a minimum period of 5 years".

3. After clause 3.2 of the said Master Circular, the following clause shall be inserted:
3.3 Stock brokers shall raise an automated alert to the security team within 15 minutes of any attempt to modify or delete order management system logs.

4. All other provisions of the Master Circular remain unchanged.

5. This circular comes into force with immediate effect.
