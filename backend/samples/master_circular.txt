SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA
CIRCULAR
SEBI/HO/MIRSD/DEMO/P/CIR/2026/101                                   November 1, 2026

To,
All Stock Brokers registered with SEBI

Subject: Master Circular on Log Retention and Access Control for Stock Brokers

1. This circular is issued to strengthen the integrity of trading systems operated by stock brokers.

2. Applicability
2.1 The provisions of this circular shall apply to all stock brokers offering internet based trading or mobile trading.

3. Log Retention
3.1 Stock brokers shall retain logs of the order management system for a minimum period of 2 years.
3.2 Such logs shall be stored in write-once-read-many (WORM) storage so that they cannot be altered.

4. Access Control
4.1 Stock brokers shall enforce multi-factor authentication for all employees accessing the back office system.
4.2 Stock brokers shall not share privileged credentials between employees.

5. Reporting
5.1 Stock brokers shall submit a quarterly compliance report on the above to the stock exchange.

6. Definitions
6.1 "Order management system" means the system used to receive, validate and route client orders.

7. This circular comes into force with immediate effect.
