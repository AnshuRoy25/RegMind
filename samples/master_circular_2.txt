SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA
CIRCULAR
SEBI/HO/MIRSD/DEMO/P/CIR/2026/102                                   November 3, 2026

To,
All Stock Brokers registered with SEBI
All recognised Stock Exchanges

Subject: Master Circular on Cyber Security for Client Facing Trading Applications

1. Background
1.1 Client facing web and mobile trading applications are a frequent target of credential theft, data leaks and account takeover attempts.
1.2 To protect investors and the integrity of the market, SEBI has decided to lay down the following baseline security requirements for such applications.

2. Applicability
2.1 The provisions of this circular shall apply to all stock brokers providing web based or mobile trading applications to clients.

3. Data Protection
3.1 Stock brokers shall encrypt all client personal and financial data at rest using AES-256 or an equivalent algorithm.
3.2 All communication between client applications and broker servers shall use TLS version 1.2 or higher.

4. Login Security
4.1 Stock brokers shall lock a client account for at least 15 minutes after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.
4.2 Mobile trading applications shall detect rooted or jailbroken devices and block trading from such devices.

5. Incident Handling and Governance
5.1 Stock brokers shall report any cyber security incident affecting client data to the stock exchange within 6 hours of detection.
5.2 Stock brokers shall appoint a Chief Information Security Officer responsible for implementation of this circular.
5.3 Stock brokers shall conduct a cyber security awareness session for all employees at least once every six months.

6. Definitions
6.1 "Client data" means any personal, financial or trading information relating to a client of the stock broker.

7. Stock exchanges are directed to bring the provisions of this circular to the notice of their members and to monitor compliance.

8. This circular comes into force with immediate effect.
