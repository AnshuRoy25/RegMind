SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA
CIRCULAR
SEBI/HO/MIRSD/DEMO/P/CIR/2026/101                                   November 1, 2026

To,
All Stock Brokers registered with SEBI
All recognised Stock Exchanges

Subject: Master Circular on Log Retention and Access Control for Stock Brokers

1. Background
1.1 Stock brokers increasingly rely on internet and mobile based trading systems. Recent inspections found gaps in how order logs are stored and in how employees access back office systems.
1.2 To strengthen the integrity and auditability of these systems, SEBI has decided to lay down the following requirements, in consultation with the stock exchanges.

2. Applicability
2.1 The provisions of this circular shall apply to all stock brokers offering internet based trading or mobile trading.

3. Log Retention
3.1 Stock brokers shall retain logs of the order management system for a minimum period of 2 years.
3.2 Such logs shall be stored in write-once-read-many (WORM) storage so that they cannot be altered.

4. Access Control
4.1 Stock brokers shall enforce multi-factor authentication for all employees accessing the back office system.
4.2 Stock brokers shall not share privileged credentials between employees.
4.3 Stock brokers shall automatically terminate inactive back office sessions after a maximum of 15 minutes of inactivity.

5. Governance and Reporting
5.1 Stock brokers shall submit a quarterly compliance report on the above to the stock exchange.
5.2 Stock brokers shall designate a senior official as the Technology Compliance Officer, who shall be responsible for implementation of this circular.
5.3 The board of directors of the stock broker shall review compliance with this circular at least once a year.

6. Definitions
6.1 "Order management system" means the system used to receive, validate and route client orders.
6.2 "Back office system" means the systems used for client onboarding, settlement, funds and securities handling.

7. Stock exchanges are directed to bring the provisions of this circular to the notice of their members and to monitor compliance.

8. This circular comes into force with immediate effect.
